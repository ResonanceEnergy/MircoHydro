## High-Priority Opportunities for Flow Cube

### National Science Foundation (NSF)

#### Improving Undergraduate STEM Education (IUSE)
- **Sponsoring Organization**: NSF Directorate for STEM Education
- **Funding Amount Range**: $200,000 - $2,000,000 per project
- **Funding Type**: Grants (up to 5 years)
- **Geographic Focus**: US institutions
- **Eligibility**: US colleges, universities, and educational organizations
- **Application Process**: Via NSF FastLane/Research.gov
- **Deadlines**: Various cycles (typically August-November)
- **Alignment with Flow Cube**: Perfect match for developing educational tools that improve STEM education, especially physics and biomimetics
- **Contact Information**: IUSE@nsf.gov
- **Success Factors**: Demonstrated educational impact, innovative approaches, broad applicability
- **Strategic Fit**: Could fund Flow Cube development as a STEM education innovation tool

#### Advancing Informal STEM Learning (AISL)
- **Sponsoring Organization**: NSF Directorate for Education and Human Resources

- **Success Factors**: Broad public impact, innovative exhibits, research-based approaches
- **Strategic Fit**: Perfect for Flow Cube museum installations and educational demonstrations
