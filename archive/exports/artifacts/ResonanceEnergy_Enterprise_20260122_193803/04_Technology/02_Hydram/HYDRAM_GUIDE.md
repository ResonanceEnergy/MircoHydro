# Hydraulic Ram Pump (Hydram) Guide — 2026-01-23

## What a hydram needs
A hydram requires **both**:
- continuous **source flow (S/Q)**, and
- a vertical **fall (F)** from source to pump site.
USAID-derived guidance notes the pump site must be below the source (e.g., ≥0.5 m) and water must be needed above the source. citeturn2search20 citeturn2search22

## Sizing relation (USAID-derived)
Life4Water’s sizing guide (citing USAID Water for the World Technical Note) provides a delivered-volume relation using an efficiency factor **E ≈ 0.66 for commercial models** (0.33 for home-built). citeturn2search20 citeturn2search22

## Design factors to capture
- Fall (source → ram)
- Lift (ram → storage/head tank)
- Source flow
- Drive pipe length/diameter (high sensitivity)
