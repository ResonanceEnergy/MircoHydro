# Hydraulics Primer (Micro-Hydro) — 2026-01-23

## Core power relation
NRCan presents a practical micro-hydro estimate: **P = Q × H × g × e**, where *e* reflects overall efficiency, typically **0.5–0.7** in real micro systems. citeturn2search1

## Headloss methods
- **Darcy–Weisbach** is broadly applicable and more accurate across fluids/conditions; it uses a friction factor and Reynolds number. citeturn2search31 citeturn2search35
- **Hazen–Williams** is an empirical water-flow method that’s simpler but limited in applicability and can be less accurate at higher velocities or different fluids. citeturn2search31 citeturn2search35
- Engineering Toolbox provides typical Hazen–Williams C coefficients by material (e.g., PVC/Plastic often 140–150). citeturn2search32

## Design rule of thumb
For early design, target **≤5–10% head loss** in penstocks and iterate diameter/length accordingly.
