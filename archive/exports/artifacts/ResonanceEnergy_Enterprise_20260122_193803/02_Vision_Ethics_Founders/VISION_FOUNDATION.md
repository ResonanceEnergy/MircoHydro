## Our three visionary inspirations (as inspiration, not proof)
### Viktor Schauberger — biomimetic hydraulics
**Use**: smooth-flow intake geometries, vortex-informed debris handling, reduced turbulence/clogging.
- Modern hydraulic literature distinguishes when swirl/vortex is beneficial vs harmful for intakes; IAHR’s monograph explicitly discusses **vortex-flow intakes** and when swirl is **undesired** for pump/hydropower intakes. citeturn2search13

### Nikola Tesla — elegance, resilience, power quality
**Use**: robust protection, blackstart, clean sine power, reliable control.
- NRCan notes micro-hydro’s reliability and site-specific design needs; our Tesla-aligned ethos is to meet codes with rigorous protection and control documentation. citeturn2search6 citeturn2search1

### Dan Winter — coherence / golden mean aesthetics (exploratory track)
**Use today (safe)**: human-centered, low-noise/low-EMI layouts; proportioning and ergonomics.
**Explore (R&D)**: catalog and evaluate geometric/phi-based ideas with measurement plans; no production claims unless validated.
- Winter’s sites and papers assert relationships between golden ratio / phase conjugation and gravity or hydrogen radii (e.g., “Planck × φ^N” framing). These are **non-mainstream** and will be maintained in a dedicated R&D annex with explicit validation gates. citeturn2search37 citeturn2search38 citeturn2search39 citeturn2search41
