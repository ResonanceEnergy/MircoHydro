# Executive Summary (v1) — 2026-01-23

## Mission
Deploy containerized, modular micro-hydro microgrids that deliver **24/7 baseline renewable power** using **gravity + natural flow** (run-of-river) with minimal ecological disruption, accelerating energy sovereignty for remote and underserved communities.

## What we’re building (Product Ladder)
- **3 kW** micro module (single-line, smallest civil footprint)
- **7.5 kW** flagship module (primary SKU)
- **15 kW** expanded module (parallelized or higher-resource sites)

Each SKU is a repeatable **containerized plant**: intake + hydram (optional) + head tank + turbine-generator + power electronics + controls + telemetry.

## Physics-first foundation (no magic)
- Micro-hydro power scales with **flow (Q)** and **head (H)**: 
  **P ≈ ρ g Q H η** and NRCan provides a practical simplified method and typical overall efficiency factor **~0.5–0.7** for micro systems. citeturn2search1 citeturn2search2
- Many micro systems operate **run-of-river** with **no large dams** and can have **low environmental impact** when designed properly. citeturn2search1 citeturn2search6
- A hydraulic ram pump (hydram) uses **fall (F)** and **source flow (S)** to lift a smaller amount of water to a higher elevation; classic USAID-derived design notes use an efficiency factor of **~0.66 for commercial** rams and relate delivered volume to **S, F, L (lift)**. citeturn2search20 citeturn2search22

## Why this is investable
- **Reliability**: Hydro provides baseline generation (day/night, winter-friendly when designed for freeze). NRCan highlights micro-hydro’s **excellent reliability** and **long life (20–30 years)** with low maintenance. citeturn2search1
- **Replicability**: Standardized container modules → faster deployment, factory acceptance testing, predictable commissioning.
- **Hybrid economics**: Batteries and PV improve resilience and peak handling; BNEF reports 2025 global average lithium-ion pack prices **$108/kWh** and stationary storage packs as low as **$70/kWh**—supporting economical microgrid stabilization. citeturn2search43
- **PV complement**: Canada PV system price benchmarks show residential turnkey prices **~2.30–3.90 CAD/W** and utility-scale systems below **~1.31 CAD/W** (context for hybrid design). citeturn2search25

## Two-pilot go-to-market
1. **Indigenous/Remote community microgrid pilot** (diesel displacement, sovereignty) — Scenario 1.
2. **Irrigation canal or industrial outfall pilot** (repeatable drop sites) — Scenario 2 or 3.

## Key deliverables in Year 1
- Flagship 7.5 kW design package + FAT test protocol
- 2 pilot deployments with data dashboards
- Costed BOM + supply chain partners
- Permitting playbook and community partnership framework
