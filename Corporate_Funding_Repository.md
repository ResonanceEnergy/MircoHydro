## Corporate Venture Capital

### Google Ventures (GV)
- **Program Name**: Google Ventures
- **Sponsoring Corporation**: Google (Alphabet Inc.)
- **Funding Amount and Type**: Equity investment ranging from $100,000 to $10 million+ per round
- **Eligibility Criteria and Focus Areas**: Early to growth-stage startups in technology sectors including clean energy, water technology, AI, and education technology. Must demonstrate strong potential for impact and scalability.
- **Application Process and Deadlines**: Rolling applications through the GV website; no fixed deadlines. Requires initial pitch deck and team introduction.
- **Strategic Fit with Flow Cube Technology**: Strong fit for educational clean tech innovations, especially those leveraging AI and data for STEM education and biomimetic designs. GV has invested in similar sustainability and edtech startups.
- **Contact Information**: https://gv.com/ (do not contact directly)
- **Track Record or Success Examples**: Portfolio includes successful exits like Nest (acquired by Google), Opower (acquired by Oracle), and numerous clean tech investments.

### Microsoft Ventures

- **Funding Amount and Type**: Equity investment from $250,000 to $50 million+; often includes Azure credits and technical support

- **Application Process and Deadlines**: Application through Microsoft for Startups portal; quarterly review cycles.
- **Strategic Fit with Flow Cube Technology**: Excellent fit for edtech platforms using cloud infrastructure and AI for physics visualization and biomimetics education.

- **Track Record or Success Examples**: Investments in companies like GitHub (acquired by Microsoft), and clean tech startups in energy efficiency.
