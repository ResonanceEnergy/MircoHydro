# Speculative — Quarantined Unvalidated-Science Material

These documents are preserved because they represent significant creative work and some may contain salvageable ideas — but **none of their technical claims are validated**, and several rest on physics that is not accepted science (golden-ratio "phase conjugation," magnetic water structuring, Schumann-resonance turbine tuning, zero-point energy).

**Do not cite, pitch, patent, or publish anything from this folder without independent experimental validation.** Mixing this material into investor decks, grant applications, or patent filings would put the credible engineering work (see `../engineering/README.md`) at risk in due diligence.
