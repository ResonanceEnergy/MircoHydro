# Vortex & Swirl for Intakes — What the literature says — 2026-01-23

## Key takeaway
IAHR’s 2023 water monograph on vortex-flow intakes explains how designers use swirl to create vortex flows (often in dropshafts), and also explicitly discusses **when swirl/vortex formation is undesired**, including for **submerged intakes used for hydropower and pumps**. citeturn2search13

## Implication for our product
- Use vortex concepts where they reduce debris/clogging and entrance loss.
