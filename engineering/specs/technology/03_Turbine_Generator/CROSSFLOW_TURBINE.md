# Crossflow Turbine & Generator Notes — 2026-01-23

## Why crossflow
Crossflow turbines are widely used in small/remote hydropower due to simplicity and cost.

## Efficiency evidence
- Adhikari & Wood’s review notes practical crossflow efficiencies typically **70–85%**, and that studies achieving **~88–90%** emphasize **full head-to-kinetic conversion in the nozzle** and **matching nozzle & runner design**. citeturn2search7
- Their nozzle redesign study reports improving a 7 kW turbine from **69% to 87%** maximum efficiency by redesigning the nozzle based on analytical/CFD methods. citeturn2search11

## Design implications for our SKU
- Treat nozzle + runner as one system.
- Provide debris-tolerant screening and maintainability.
