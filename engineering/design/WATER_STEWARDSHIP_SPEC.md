**The stewardship claim stack, per canon:** nothing is said publicly until the DO/turbidity/temperature deltas are measured on the demo unit. When they are, "the machine that gives the river back better water" is a claim **zero competitors can make** (receipts files: nobody even publishes efficiency curves, let alone water-quality deltas) — and it is grant-channel gold.

## Adopted into design (measured-mechanism basis)

1. **Egg headstock with tangential top entry** — D-8, ratified. Settling + de-airation + no-dead-zone mixing (industry-vindicated geometry). Bottom silt drain valve added to the vessel spec (returns settled material to the river bed downstream of the intake — closes the sediment loop without a turbine pass).
2. **Discharge arrangement:** tailrace enters the river as a spread riffle (rock apron), not a pipe jet — final aeration step + zero bank scour. (Also Schauberger's prescription; adopted for the measured aeration + erosion reasons.)
3. **Copper, selectively [option]:** headstock wetted fittings and the silt-drain in copper alloy where biofouling matters (EPA-measured antimicrobial). NOT structural copper everywhere — cost says no, and the antimicrobial case only justifies contact surfaces. Flagged as northern-package option (biofilm control in low-maintenance installs).
4. **Temperature discipline:** already ours via REQ-P5 (insulated/buried); formally note the summer corollary — shade the headstock, don't let delivery water sun-soak. Free.
5. **D-6 rig gains ΔP-vs-flow instrumentation** across the fluted pipe — the modern, publishable version of the 1952 Stuttgart experiment, run honestly on our hardware.
