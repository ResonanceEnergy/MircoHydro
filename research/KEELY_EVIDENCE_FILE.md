# John Worrell Keely — Neutral Evidence File

**Date:** 2026-07-18 · **Status:** replaces the verdict-first Keely paragraph in earlier research (founder directive: present the record without prejudice). This file compiles documented claims, witnesses, physical evidence on both sides, and open questions — and ends with what would settle them, not with a verdict.

## The sympathetic record (sourced)

- **Signed scientist statements (April 8, 1890),** reproduced in Bloomfield-Moore's *Keely and His Discoveries* (1893, [full text](https://archive.org/details/keelyhisdiscover00moorrich)): Prof. **Joseph Leidy** (U. Penn): *"he has fairly demonstrated the discovery of a force previously unknown to science… I believe Mr. Keely to be honest."* **James M. Willcox, Ph.D.**: *"forces that cannot be explained by any known physical laws."*
- **Prof. W. Lascelles-Scott** (London), after a month-long 1896 investigation, told the Franklin Institute: *"Keely has demonstrated to me, in a way which is absolutely unquestionable, the existence of a force hitherto unknown."*
- The **580-lb lever test** (engineer's account: ~18,900 psi against the piston; lever rose "as if the great mass of iron had been only cork" and resisted added body weight); the 1897 demonstration before the Manhattan Elevated GM and Western Union chief engineer (who "reported surprise at the force produced"); the claim (Bloomfield-Moore) that witnesses observed 10,000–20,000 psi produced *without heat* — argued by supporters as incompatible with compressed air (adiabatic heating).
- Rebuttals to the 1899 exposé from Keely Motor Co. president Ackerman (tubes = discarded pre-1887 relics) and shop-man Schuellerman; period "practical machinery men" noted **no compressor plant capable of the claimed pressures existed in the 1870s** (leading expert Hoadly's steel bottles burst at 14,000 psi) — an argument that, note, cuts against Keely's own claimed figures equally.

## The skeptical record (sourced)

- **Lt. E.L. Zalinski** at the 1884 Sandy Hook gun test: "none of the experiments… showed that Keely had discovered a new force"; his offered 10,000-psi gauge was refused ("I do not believe in pressure gauges, anyhow").
- Engineers **E.A. Scott and A.B. Burk**, given repeated access from Nov. 1895, concluded compressed air; Scott hypothesized the "solid wire" was a capillary tube and the levitation flask a Cartesian diver. When Mrs. Moore proposed the decisive **wire-cutting test, Keely refused** — and she cut his stipend.
- **1888:** rather than fully demonstrate the motor to court-appointed experts, Keely went to Moyamensing Prison for contempt (reversed on appeal as premature).
- **Jan. 1899 posthumous investigation** ([Scientific American, "The Keely Motor Fraud"](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/the-keely-motor-fraud/); Philadelphia Press, Jan. 19, 1899): a three-ton hollow steel sphere in the basement, brass/steel tubing in walls and floors, a worn steel shaft through the ceiling, a plugged floor tube beneath the rotating-globe position.

## What complicates BOTH records

- **The investigation was adversarial and the lab was empty.** Lead investigator **Clarence Moore was Clara's estranged son, in active litigation over her estate, with a stated financial interest** in discrediting Keely ("rather than let the estate go…I shall certainly combat the will") and a declared prior conviction ("never had any faith… started by me to see whether I was right"). The apparatus had already shipped to Boston; Moore himself conceded the dismantling "greatly lessened" the chances of a satisfactory investigation. **The team documented *means* of deception; it reproduced no demonstration.** The sphere's feed tube was rusted and unconnected.
- **Kinraide's silence.** Keely's chosen successor took 20 crates of apparatus, dismissed the tube findings in January, quit in May 1899 citing "notoriety," and — asked directly if the motor was a fraud — "replied that he had not arrived at any such conclusion." The only claim that he privately duplicated the effects with compressed air/springs/magnets is **secondhand, anonymous, and relayed through the hostile Moore**. His glass-plate negatives of the machines were rediscovered in his house's hidden rooms (Behary, electrotherapymuseum.org) and remain largely unpublished.
- **The patent record is thinner than legend either way:** one real granted patent ([US 118,022, 1871 flywheel](https://patents.google.com/patent/US118022A/en)) and one **abandoned 1872 US application** (Hydro Vacuo Engine — spec survives; Patent Office demanded a working model that was never supplied). Not "refused to patent on principle"; not "patented his discoveries" either.
- **Physics adjacencies exist in kind, never in magnitude:** sound moving matter predates Keely (Kundt 1866); acoustic levitation is real at milligram–gram scale (Bücks & Müller 1933 → modern arrays); cavitation and water hammer (his first engine was literally a water-driven "hydro-vacuo" device) produce locally enormous transient pressures; sympathetic resonance of tuned structures is textbook. **No modern analogue exists** for net energy extraction from resonance, acoustic water dissociation, or gravity nullification.
- **Modern replications:** Dale Pond's Musical Dynasphere replicas (Atlin, 1996 →) are physically built and beautiful; the builders themselves state **"We have not yet achieved full rotation"** and publish no instrumented measurements. The evidence base is testimonial, positive or null.

## Open questions (unresolved on the record)

1. Can the well-attested demonstrations (the lever test, wire-transmitted disk rotation, no-heat pressure "liberation") be *quantitatively* reproduced with 1870s–90s hidden-plant technology? Nobody has run the feasibility study in either direction.
2. What do Kinraide's plates and estate papers contain? Was a "Kinraide on Keely" report ever drafted?
3. Where did the machinery go after Jamaica Plain? (Its return to Philadelphia was announced, never recorded.)
4. Do Bloomfield-Moore's strongest set-pieces have corroboration outside her book and friendly reprints?

## What would settle it

- **Industrial CT / borescope the two surviving artifacts** — the "Etheric Force Machine" at the [American Precision Museum](https://www.neh.gov/humanities/2010/mayjune/curio/the-etheric-force-machine) (Windsor, VT) and the [Smithsonian tubing (acc. 1981.0383)](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_849065): are the "solid wires" capillary tubes? Hidden check valves? *The cheapest decisive test available today.*
- Publish the Kinraide plates; search the Jamaica Plain/Behary archives for 1899 lab notes.
- Pull the primary files: Philadelphia Press Jan 19–20 1899 full text, *Wilson v. Keely* inspection notes, the 1872 application wrapper at NARA.
- A two-direction engineering feasibility study (period hidden-plant capability vs Keely's own claimed pressures against period materials).
- Any dynasphere on an instrumented, shielded test stand — converting 130 years of testimony into a measurement, whichever way it falls.
