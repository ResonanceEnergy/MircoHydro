## What mainstream physics accepts, mapped

- **Vortex/swirl aeration measurably raises dissolved oxygen** ([Water 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/w14050771), [ACS Omega 2024](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsomega.4c08452)) — the real, defensible core of "living water is oxygen-rich after spiraling." It's aeration, not life-force. **[M]**
- **Egg tanks**: adopted at industrial scale for no-dead-zone mixing and sediment handling. **[M]**
- **Curved/helical/oval pipe drag reduction below straight pipe**: measured, 2025. **[M]** — this is the honest modern test of the founder's fluted penstock too (D-6 ΔP instrumentation).
- **Copper antimicrobial**: EPA-registered surface efficacy. **[M]**
- **What remains lore**: over-unity/implosion energy, levitation, durable water "memory" (H-bond networks reorganize in **picoseconds** — [Nature 2005](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature03383)), drop-pictures/crystal photos as assays (failed triple-blind replication), commercial "structured water." **[L]**
