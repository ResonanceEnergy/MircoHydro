## Verdict, honestly scored against the founder's challenge

**~80–95% of the claim holds, and the exceptions are named:**

1. **"Century-old designs sold for water delivery" — CONFIRMED** at every price tier on five continents. Green & Carter literally sells the 1796 lineage with a forever guarantee; Billabong, Rife, Walton, Schlumpf, Marumby are all heritage-pattern weighted-clack machines marketed on longevity, never on measured performance.
2. **"Cast iron" — OVERSTATED (correction to our own framing).** The 2026 market is materially diverse: PVC ($95 LTH), steel (€349 fuxus, Chinese imports), stainless (Zahner CHF-class), cast aluminum (Ram Co. 3"), and two genuine modern composites (Papa Pump's injection-moulded springless-elastomer design; HydroHammer's £600 composite). Cast iron rules only the premium heritage tier.
3. **"Shipped untuned" — CONFIRMED with the best receipts in the file.** Billabong's own brochure: *"valve compensation cannot be pre-set at the factory."* Zahner sells the adjustment device as a **CHF 310 optional extra** and disclaims any guarantee of delivered volume. Land To House ships a fixed swing check with zero adjustment. No vendor on Earth ships a factory-characterized or performance-guaranteed ram. (Gota Terra's 1/2/3-valve factory variants are the closest thing to productized tuning — coarse configuration, not commissioning.)
4. **"Nobody sells sensor-equipped" — FALSIFIED by exactly one vendor.** HydroHammer (UK): Smart Hydram £4,300 + subscription, HARP retrofit £150–300, digital start/stop/throttle. Scale caveat is real (2020 micro-startup, placeholder text in shop policies, zero published performance data) — a proof-of-existence, not a market segment. **What they sell is aperture/throttle control — not per-cycle timing, not phase-locking, not efficiency optimization.** Our narrowed IP frame (zero-recoil phase-lock, bank coordination, generation-optimized control) survives this receipt intact.
5. **"Nobody sells generation-oriented" — DENTED, not destroyed.** WPT markets the Venturo for electricity generation explicitly — as a bespoke survey/lease arrangement, ~8 years running without a catalog product or a published install. One Chinese listing uses generation language in copy. **No off-the-shelf generation ram exists anywhere.**

**Business read:** the worldwide receipts confirm the two-model comparison's premise — everywhere volume exists (China $730 cast iron, Brazil R$4k, Indonesia $15, Philippines ₱7.5k+), the product is the untuned classic, and the tuning that doubles output (our measured 2.5×) is left to the installer or sold as a CHF 310 afterthought. The gap we're building into is real, and its two named competitors (HydroHammer, WPT) are both UK micro-companies with no published performance data.
