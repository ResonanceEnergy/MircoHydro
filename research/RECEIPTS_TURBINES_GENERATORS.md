## Verdict against the claim — CONFIRMED, all four gaps, with receipts

1. **Buyable off the shelf: TRUE.** Real carts, real prices: $1,650 (Langston) to $5,445 (Scott) Western; NZ$2,330–5,244 (PowerSpout); ~$10–15k Chinese packaged low-head kits; $250–700 assembles a complete DIY pico powertrain from Motenergy parts. Our $750 powertrain floor stands verified.
2. **Low-head <10 m: PARTIALLY CLOSED, thinly.** PowerSpout LH (1–5 m) and ES&D LH1000 (<3 m) are genuinely buyable. But between ~2 kW and Turbulent's 15 kW/700 L/s floor, the 1–10 m band is quote-only or empty — and Solacity (Canada) already lists the LH as discontinued. **The band our machines target is served by exactly two small vendors with wobbling distribution.**
3. **NA grid certification: FULLY CONFIRMED GAP.** Not one UL1741/cCSAus-listed hydro unit exists anywhere. Every grid path (Scott's $7,975 package, PowerSpout's approved list) piggybacks on PV-certified string inverters without addressing certification scope. Matches our REQ-S2 posture exactly.
4. **Cold climate: FULLY CONFIRMED GAP.** The industry's best cold-weather offering is a PowerSpout blog post (watch below −7 °C, one dealer anecdote at −27 °C) — no vendor sells winterization hardware, even those retailing through Fairbanks. REQ-P5 has no competition.
5. **Honest efficiency data: CONFIRMED, and starker than we claimed.** Across every vendor on four continents — Suneco, Forster, Greef, Galaxy, Top Grand, Motenergy, Hurricane, Missouri Wind, Turbiwatt, Turbulent, Ossberger, Scott — **zero published measured partial-load efficiency curves.** The single credible measured water-to-wire number in the world market (50%, range 42–56%) comes from a customer's instrumented blog, not any manufacturer — and it sits far below the 60–87% marketing numbers. Alxion (industrial, quote-only) is the lone data-publishing exception.
6. **Market fragility receipt:** PowerPal discontinued (distributor domain for sale), Zeropex dead (domain for sale), Scott dropped by resellers, Solacity lists LH discontinued. Niche players die quietly in this market — the durability argument for our appliance+fleet-telemetry model writes itself.

**What Resonance buys vs builds (receipts-backed):** BUY the PMG ($179), wheels ($40–95), MPPT ($471 SRNE), pipe/couplings, bladder vessel. BUILD the ram tuning/control, the system coupling, the cold package, the certification path, and the honest published curves — because the receipts show, vendor by vendor, that none of those five can be purchased at any price.
