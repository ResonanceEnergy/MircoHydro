## Core equations
Approximate mechanical power:

```text
P ≈ ρ g Q H η

ρ ≈ 1000 kg/m³, g ≈ 9.81 m/s²
Q = flow (m³/s)
H = net head (m)
η = overall efficiency (turbine+generator+drive+losses)
```

## Practical design steps
1) Measure gross head
2) Estimate design flow and seasonal minimum
3) Calculate penstock losses and net head
4) Select turbine class (impulse vs reaction) and generator
5) Design intake, screening, trash rack, and sediment handling
6) Electrical integration (dump load / inverter / battery / grid‑forming)
