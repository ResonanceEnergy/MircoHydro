## Off‑grid guidance
Hydrogen can be a long‑duration storage pathway but adds complexity (compression/storage, safety, conversion losses). For most small off‑grid systems, batteries are simpler; hydrogen becomes interesting when seasonal storage or fuel logistics dominate.
