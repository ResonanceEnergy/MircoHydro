## What the internal files actually say

1. **Canon (FULL_SYSTEM_DESIGN, ratified):** headstock = 4 m³ at EL +9.0, vented, overflow to stream; three jobs — pressure reference, surge/boost storage, **air separation & silt settling**. **Shape: unspecified.** (The box tank in earlier renders was the artist's default, not a spec.)
2. **Founder V-Next lineage (names survive; contents lost in the data wipe):** `10_Design_VNext/01_Tank_Inlet_Hyperbolic/` — *Hyperbolic_Inlet_Design*, *Engineering_Rationale_Hyperbolic_Inlet*, plus `05_Tank_Hydraulics_Unit/TANK_Inlet_Comparison_RUN2` — the founder was **designing and comparatively testing tank inlet geometries**, with a hyperbolic (vortex-funnel) inlet as the V-Next direction. The docx shells are empty (INTERNAL_DATA_AUDIT) — the drawings are gone, the intent is not.
3. **Founder collections:** the Schauberger corpus in-repo lists the **copper egg** among his key innovations; the visionary roadmap specifies an "intake vortex settling tank — conical bottom, vortex-inducing inlet, >90% sediment removal." **T-002 (tank ripple) was literally built as the metric for these inlet comparisons.**

**Answer to the founder's question: yes — the design lineage points to an egg/ovoid vessel with a swirl-inducing top inlet, and the box tank was a substitution by omission. Corrected.**

## What is decided

1. **Headstock geometry: ovoid (egg) vessel, pointed end down**, on a cradle stand at EL +9.0; volume per canon (~4 m³ class).
2. **Entry at the TOP**: rise pipe discharges over/into the top collar (tangential/hyperbolic entry per V-Next lineage). Engineering benefits that don't depend on any contested theory: an over-the-top discharge gives an air gap (no back-siphon to the ram — removes a check valve), the falling/tangential entry de-airates delivery water, the ovoid's converging lower profile concentrates settled silt at a single bottom drain point, and the penstock offtake draws from the settled, air-free region above it. The egg has no flat panels — structurally the best pressure/freeze shape per unit wall.
3. **Test obligation (canon discipline):** the shape's claimed settling/coherence benefits are measured, not asserted — **T-002 tank-ripple + a settling comparison (ovoid+tangential vs box+plain) join the bench roster**, exactly the RUN2 comparison the founder had started before the data loss. Jet-side judgment stays with T-001 CoV (D-6 rig shared).
4. All three visualization scenes updated (wide, flythrough, powerhouse); engineering drawing of the vessel + inlet goes on the P1 drawing package list.
