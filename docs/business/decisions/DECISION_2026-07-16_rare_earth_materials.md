# Decision: Rare-Earth Materials Ban — Rescinded

**Date:** 2026-07-16 · **Decided by:** Founder (NATRIX) · **Status:** RATIFIED
**Resolves:** Founding Principles Audit, Theme E tension + Priority Action #3 (`docs/strategy/FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES_AUDIT.md`)

## Decision

The blanket ban on rare-earth elements in `ETHICS_VALUES_BRAND.md` ("Avoid rare earth elements and conflict materials") is **rescinded**. Rare-earth materials — specifically NdFeB permanent magnets in generators/alternators — are permitted in all product lines.

## Context

The ban conflicted with both machine designs: Machine A's direct-drive PMSG and Machine B's permanent-magnet alternator depend on neodymium magnets. Alternatives (ferrite-magnet machines, capacitor-excited induction generators, wound-rotor synchronous) cost 3–5+ points of system efficiency, add size/weight, or add brush maintenance — each of which works against stronger founding principles: simplicity (CV3), durability/serviceability (CV2), 24/7 reliability (North Star), and design-for-ROI (CV7). The audit identified this as a genuine values-vs-engineering trade requiring an explicit founder call.

## Conditions retained (the spirit survives)

1. **Conflict-materials avoidance stands** unchanged.
2. **Responsible sourcing:** traceable magnet supply chain preferred as the company scales.
3. **Circularity:** magnet recovery is added to the end-of-life / take-back program (magnets are among the most recoverable high-value components in the BOM).
4. Battery chemistry remains **LiFePO₄ (cobalt-free)** — unaffected by this decision.

## Effects

- `ETHICS_VALUES_BRAND.md` §Environmental Commitments amended (amendment noted inline).
- Founding Principles Audit Theme E verdict: ⚠️ TENSION → ✅ RESOLVED BY DECISION.
- Machine A and Machine B generator selections proceed as designed (PMSG / PM alternator).
- The ferrite/induction option remains documented as a cost-down or supply-risk fallback, not a values requirement.
