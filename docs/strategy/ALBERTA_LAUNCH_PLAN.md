# Alberta Launch Plan — Requirements, Phased Path, Alternatives

**Date:** 2026-07-18 · **Premise:** founder is Alberta-born, Alberta-based, with an operating HDD (directional drilling) business — local presence, field crews, fabrication contacts, and rural credibility. This is the ground-up plan to launch via the Canadian grant model (`../market/CANADA_GRANT_MODEL.md`) with commercial alternatives running in parallel.

## Founder assets worth naming

Alberta residency and Treaty-territory presence (relationship channel); **HDD capability is directly synergistic** — directional drilling installs penstocks and water lines under roads, berms, and sensitive banks without open cuts, which is precisely the civil work micro-hydro and cistern-supply projects need; existing WCB/insurance/corporate infrastructure via the drilling business; equipment and shop access.

## PART 1 — REQUIREMENTS (the complete checklist)

### A. Corporate & administrative
1. **Alberta corporation** for the energy venture (separate from HDD co. for liability; ~$450 registration + NUANS). Consider name aligned to brand decision (X3 canon pending).
2. CRA business number, GST registration.
3. **Insurance:** CGL ($2–5M) + product liability once units ship; E&O if doing site assessments for others. WCB account (or extend existing).
4. **Indigenous Business Directory readiness:** plan the JV shape early — either a band-owned dev corp as prime with us as OEM (their 51%+), or a formal JV where the Indigenous partner performs ≥33% of work value. This is a *structure decision per project*, not a one-time filing.
5. Federal supplier registration (SAP Ariba/PSPC) for direct procurement later; MERX/Bids&Tenders watch for band RFPs.

### B. Product & certification
6. **Electrical:** all grid-facing electronics must carry Canadian certification (cCSAus / CSA C22.2 No. 107.1 class inverters — the commodity solar path already planned). Off-grid DC systems still need permits under the Canadian Electrical Code via local inspection (Alberta: municipal/agency electrical permits).
7. **CSA field evaluation (SPE-1000)** for the assembled unit if sold as an appliance — budget ~$3–8k per model; plan at productization, not prototype.
8. **Validation gate (self-imposed, non-negotiable):** no community pitch with performance numbers until the digital bench (MOC water-hammer sim) and a physical measurement back them. The honesty principle is also a sales requirement in this channel — these communities have been burned by vendors before.

### C. Water & environmental (Alberta)
9. **Water Act (Alberta):** hydro use of water = a diversion requiring a **licence** from Alberta Environment and Protected Areas — even non-consumptive ([approvals guide](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/6f89990b-6088-4ed6-9df9-4b0d609e7c88/resource/03e85d8c-20dc-48dc-b28f-5928e84ee07b/download/epa-guide-preparing-water-act-application-for-licence-or-approval-2024-05.pdf), [fact sheet](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/aa7b6ac2-0868-4f4d-8f78-193c1bff28fa/resource/35e36668-de5c-40ec-b3cb-226bc54cd8ec/download/aenv-water-act-fact-sheet-approvals-and-licences-7511.pdf)). Key nuances:
   - **Household purposes exemption (up to 1,250 m³/yr)** and **registered traditional agriculture use (6,250 m³/yr)** exist but are volume-based — a ram or turbine *returning* all water may still be treated as a diversion; strategy: pre-file a **regulatory pathway memo** with AEPA for a non-consumptive, damless, fully-returned micro diversion and seek a standardized treatment. Getting AEPA to bless a template is itself a moat (permits-in-box, Alberta edition).
   - **Basin closure:** South Saskatchewan basin (most of southern AB) is closed to new allocations — transfers only. **Athabasca and Peace basins (northern AB — where the Indigenous/remote work is) are open.** Site strategy follows the water law map.
   - On-reserve projects: federal lands — ISC/federal processes rather than provincial in some elements; the community's technical services (TSAG) navigate this.
10. **Fisheries:** DFO authorization self-assessment; our damless, screened (≤0.3 m/s), full-return design is built to pass the low-risk pathway. Document it per site (DFO-ready dossier in the box).
11. **Grid-tie (only if/when relevant):** Alberta's **Micro-generation Regulation** — renewable, offsets own load, ≤5 MW; ≤150 kW = "small micro" with a simple notice via the wire owner (Fortis/ATCO), AUC guideline current ([alberta.ca](https://www.alberta.ca/micro-generation), [AUC 2026 guideline](https://media.auc.ab.ca/prd-wp-uploads/regulatory_documents/Reference/MicrogenerationNoticeSubmissionGuideline2026.pdf)). Off-grid = none of this applies.

### D. Partnership & program (the grant channel)
12. **Community as applicant** (band council resolution) — we are technology partner. Requirements on us: capacity to co-develop the application, transparent pricing, training plan, local-hiring content, OCAP-respectful data handling, and patience.
13. **Gatekeeper relationships (Alberta-first):** **TSAG** (Treaty-area technical services; runs Alberta's Circuit Rider water training — the bridge to the water file), **AIOC** ecosystem, **ICE** (20/20 Catalysts network), FNPA. Fort Chipewyan's **Three Nations Energy** (ACFN + Mikisew Cree + Fort Chip Métis) is Alberta's flagship off-grid precedent — Canada's largest off-grid solar — and the most sophisticated potential first conversation in the province.
14. **Program filings:** Wah-ila-toos/Northern REACHE (open now, sunsets March 2027) require: community applicant, project description, budget, community support evidence, capacity plan. Feasibility-stage ask first ($150–500k class), capital second.

## PART 2 — THE PLAN (phased, gated)

### Phase 0 — Foundation (now → 3 months) · cost ~$5–10k
- Incorporate; insurance quotes; brand/name decision (canon X3).
- **Digital bench:** build the MOC water-hammer simulator, replace the η_ram fit, regenerate all Machine B numbers. *Gate 0: simulated efficiency within the published ram envelope — or redesign before anyone sees a claim.*
- Assemble the **Alberta regulatory pathway memo** (Water Act + DFO, both product lines) and open the AEPA conversation.
- Resolve design decisions D-1..D-5 so the pitch shows one machine, not options.

### Phase 1 — Relationships + first physical proof (months 2–9) · cost ~$10–25k
- Join/attend: ICE gatherings, TSAG events, Treaty 8 energy forums; one honest capability one-pager (validated numbers only).
- Approach order: **TSAG intro → 2–3 Treaty 8 communities with active water/energy pain → Three Nations Energy conversation** (partner/pilot host or advisor). Northern SK/NWT border communities as the diesel-belt extension (REACHE-eligible).
- Build one **demonstration unit at a private Alberta site** (foothills ranch/acreage; household-exemption-friendly) — the machine must be seeable and touchable before any community commits. HDD crew installs it. *Gate 1: measured water-to-wire within ±10 pts of model (the repo's own validation requirement).*
- Identify the community **champion** (the actual buyer) in each target community.

### Phase 2 — First funded feasibility (months 6–18) · grant-funded
- Co-develop one Wah-ila-toos/REACHE **capacity/feasibility application** with the lead community (file before the March 2027 sunset window pressure peaks). Parallel: Yukon IREI application with a YT community if one emerges via ICE network (open, rolling, 75%).
- Scope: the **northern package** (power + heat + ram water-lift + optional greenhouse), site survey with consent/community columns, training plan via Circuit Rider linkage for the water side.
- *Gate 2: one signed community partnership + one funded feasibility. If zero by month 18 → weight shifts to Alternatives track (below), channel stays warm.*

### Phase 3 — Demo build (months 18–36) · grant-funded capital + ITC
- Capital application (deployment stream, up to 100%); band-owned corp structure captures **30% Clean Tech ITC** refund on top.
- Build with local hires + HDD crew; telemetry from day one; publish performance openly (the dashboard commitment from the founding docs — it finally activates here).
- *Gate 3: unit performing to spec through one full winter = the reference install that unlocks every subsequent community and the institutional dataset.*

### Budget reality
Founder cash exposure across Phases 0–1: **~$15–35k** (entity, insurance, sim work, one private demo unit, travel). Everything beyond that is designed to be grant-carried or revenue-carried. Time is the real cost: this channel pays in years 2–4, not year 1 — which is exactly why Part 3 runs in parallel.

## PART 3 — ALTERNATIVES (parallel and fallback)

**Alt A — Private Alberta market (runs in parallel from day one).** Ram-alone (water) sales to foothills ranchers/acreages — livestock water without fuel or power, household-exemption-friendly, HDD customer base overlaps directly. Kit and kit+ram sales to off-grid cabins/outfitters in the foothills and BC-border country. This is the revenue-now track and the source of demo sites.

**Alt B — Alberta irrigation districts (institutional, non-Indigenous).** The 13 districts' canal drop structures; provincial jurisdiction, district-owned conduits, ACRE3-style precedent funding. Slower procurement but zero remoteness cost. Natural second institutional channel; the Site Atlas's first mapping target.

**Alt C — Water-file-first entry.** If energy-file timelines drag: lead with the **ram as water infrastructure** through TSAG/Circuit Rider relationships (ISC FNWWEP is renewed at $2.3B/3yr and politically protected). A $2–5k zero-power water lift is a smaller, faster community decision than an energy project — and it builds the trust that energy projects later ride on.

**Alt D — Adjacent-territory jump.** Yukon IREI (open, rolling, hydro-explicit) and NWT communities via REACHE if Alberta community timelines stall — Alberta base, territorial projects; normal pattern for northern contractors.

**Alt E — Pure-commercial fallback.** If the grant channel underdelivers by Gate 2: full pivot to the NA private market plan (lodges, hatcheries via the US conduit path, homesteads) already documented in `MARKET_MAP_LOW_HEAD.md`. Nothing built for the grant channel is wasted — the winterized product and the honest dataset are the same assets.
