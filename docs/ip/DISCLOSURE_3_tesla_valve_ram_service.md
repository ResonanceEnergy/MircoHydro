# Invention Disclosure 3 — Tesla-Valve Auxiliary Functions in Hydraulic Ram Service
**Date of record:** 2026-07-19 · **Inventor:** NATRIX · **Status:** internal draft; evidence pending bench (A/B slots reserved).
**Title:** Use of fixed-geometry valvular conduits (Tesla valves) as snifter and delivery-line transient damping elements in hydraulic ram pumps.
**The invention:** replacing the failure-prone moving-part snifter with a Tesla-valve conduit performing asymmetric air admission, and/or placing a valvular conduit in the delivery line as a passive transient damper — exploiting the ram's ~1 Hz pulsed-flow regime where measured diodicity is highest (up to 2.5x quasi-steady).
**Prior art distinguished:** US1,329,559 (expired) is the conduit itself; Nat. Comm. 2021 measured diodicity; NASA studied hammer mitigation — but the ram-service combination is unpublished (verified searches, RAM_RESONANCE_RESEARCH §5). 
**Evidence needed before filing:** bench A/B (air admission rate vs drilled/check snifter; delivery-line pressure ripple with/without conduit) — D-6 rig add-on.
