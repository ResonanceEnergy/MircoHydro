# Canada — Grant Model for Underserved Reserves, Northern Living & Water Supply

**Date:** 2026-07-18 · Verified program status as of July 2026; sources inline. Companion to `MARKET_MAP_LOW_HEAD.md`.

## The strategic finding

Three separately-funded federal files converge on one northern community — **energy** (diesel reduction), **water** (safe drinking water), and **food security** — and our product family is the only vendor class that touches all three from one stream: the turbine/heat side rides the energy programs, the zero-power ram rides the water file, and the heated-greenhouse load module rides the food file. One creek → power + heat + water lift + greenhouse, funded from three different pots. No competitor (solar, diesel, or hydro) can stitch that pitch.

## 2. The water file — where the ram-alone product lands

- **38 long-term drinking-water advisories in 36 communities remain** (June 2026); 156 lifted since 2015 ([ISC live list](https://www.sac-isc.gc.ca/eng/1614387410146/1614387435325)).
- **Bill C-37, First Nations Clean Water Act** — reintroduced June 2026 (successor to C-61, which died on prorogation): national standards + a co-developed *funding framework*. Not yet law; watch it ([ISC](https://www.canada.ca/en/indigenous-services-canada/news/2026/06/bill-c-37-first-nations-clean-water-act.html), [AFN](https://afn.ca/all-news/bulletins/bill-c-37-the-first-nations-clean-water-act/)).
- **$6B** committed for reliable safe drinking water under the 2021 settlement (build-out lagging — litigation pressure keeps this file politically protected); **$2.3B/3yr FNWWEP renewal in Budget 2025** (~800 active projects); **Circuit Rider** operator-training >$12M/yr via tribal-council technical orgs.
- **The technical wedge:** many Prairie/northern reserves run on **trucked water to cisterns** (documented contamination risk) and diesel-powered source/booster pumping. A hydraulic ram lifting source water to a cistern, treatment intake, or header tank — **zero power, zero fuel, no operator, −40 °C-hardened** — maps directly onto the gap between "treatment plant funded" and "distribution/source conveyance unsolved." Honest boundary: rams convey **raw** water; potable treatment/certification stays with the existing water-plant scope. We move water; we don't treat it.

## 3. Northern living conditions — the demand numbers

292 diesel-dependent remote communities (confirmed; [Pembina](https://www.pembina.org/pub/diesel-true-cost)); ~178 off both grid and gas ([CER](https://www.cer-rec.gc.ca/en/data-analysis/energy-markets/market-snapshots/2023/market-snapshot-clean-energy-projects-remote-indigenous-northern-communities.html)); true power costs to $1.14/kWh (Nunavut). Heating: 2,000–3,500 L oil/yr per household ≈ **$3,000–6,000+/yr** — the heat-dump load module's exact target. Food insecurity: Nunavut households 46–57% — and CanNor is actively funding community food projects ([$1.75M NWT, Mar 2026](https://www.canada.ca/en/northern-economic-development/news/2026/03/backgrounder-cannor-invests-over-175-million-in-community-led-food-security-projects-in-the-northwest-territories.html)) — the heated-greenhouse load module rides this third file.

## 4. How a vendor actually gets in

1. **The community is the applicant, we are the technology partner.** Funding flows as contribution agreements to band councils / band-owned dev corps; band council resolution starts everything.
2. **JV structure for procurement:** Indigenous Business Directory tests — ≥51% Indigenous owned/controlled, or JV where the Indigenous partner performs ≥33% of work value — also unlocks the federal **5% Indigenous procurement target** outside grant channels. Practical shape: band dev-corp as prime, us as OEM/licensor; assembly work localized (aligns with EVB local-manufacturing value).
3. **Gatekeepers to court:** tribal-council technical services (**OFNTSC** Ontario, **TSAG** Alberta, **FNPA** power-specific), and the **champions** — ICE 20/20 Catalysts cohorts, IESO-salaried Community Energy Champions, the 24 IODI champions. These individuals initiate projects; they are the market.
4. **Precedent exists:** Atlin (Taku River Tlingit, 100% FN-owned hydro, off diesel since 2009), Hesquiaht micro-hydro (CERRC-funded), Kokish JV — hydro on reserves is a known, celebrated pattern, not a novelty pitch.
5. **Honest timeline:** energy plan → feasibility → design/permits (water licence + DFO fish review — our damless, screened, low-head designs are built for this) → build: **3–5 years concept-to-commissioning** even for small projects. The pre-packaged regulatory file (permits-in-box, northern edition) is the main compression lever.

## 6. Program design implications for us

- **File a demo into Wah-ila-toos/REACHE in 2026** (authority sunsets March 2027; renewal is a Budget 2026-27 decision — being in the queue beats waiting).
- **The northern package is the product:** one stream → turbine (power) + heat-dump (offsets $3–6k/yr oil) + ram (water lift) + greenhouse (food) — each element fundable from its own file. Never pitch a turbine; pitch the package.
- **No bitcoin module in this channel, ever** (already ruled): battery+loads, heat, greenhouse, and productive-use only.
- **Founding-principles fit:** community-as-applicant, co-ownership, FPIC, training — the ⏸ premature commitments in the principles audit (Theme C) all activate in this channel; the site-survey consent columns become mandatory here.
- **Design deltas for north:** −40 °C package (already analyzed), cistern-integration kit for the ram (float-valve header, insulated dog-house, heat-trace option), DFO-ready screened intake documentation.
