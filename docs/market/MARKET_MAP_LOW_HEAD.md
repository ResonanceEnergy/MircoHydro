# Market Map — Low-Head Gap, Residential + Commercial Routes

**Date:** 2026-07-16 · **Scope:** where paying demand co-locates with our site type (0.5–2 m fall, 60–200+ L/s → Machine B 0.2–1.4 kW; 3–10 m → Machine A 2.5–4 kW). Four regions, government + private, sourced.

## The one-line answer

**The money is in the commercial route; the residential route is the beachhead.** Commercial anchors (tea factories, hatcheries, lodges, irrigation districts) pay full price today from diesel/battery savings; government programs (Canada, Nepal, Philippines, Peru) pay 70–100% of capex but move on 2–3 year cycles; residential North America is small ($10–40M SAM) but immediate, full-margin, and builds the brand and install base.

## ROUTE 2 — COMMERCIAL / INSTITUTIONAL (ranked)

### Tier 1 — Direct sale, full price, 0–12 months

1. **African tea estates & factories.** KTDA (Kenya) alone: 54 smallholder factories, energy ≈ 30% of opex, KSh 30–65M/yr power spend each, four hydro plants already built and bills cut ~50% — proven appetite, complaints about stalled bigger projects = opening for small/fast/cheap units. Same play: Tanzania (Mwenga/Rift Valley Energy precedent), Malawi (MEGA, Mulanje). Many estates sit on the low-head streams we serve. *Payer: factory opex. Price: full.*
2. **NA aquaculture & fish hatcheries.** 3,453 US farms ($1.9B sales) + ~70 federal and hundreds of state hatcheries; flow-through raceways are literally our site (constant 100+ L/s, 0.5–3 m drops, 24/7 loads) **and are man-made conduits → FERC license-free (NOI, ~45 days)**; private farms can stack REAP 25–50% grants when the window reopens. Nobody markets to this segment. *Payer: farm owner / state capital budget.*
3. **Remote lodges (Canada/Alaska/Africa).** Diesel at $1–2/kWh effective; lodge owners already write $60–190k solar-retrofit checks; hydro adds silence + 24/7 + shoulder-season power. *Payer: owner. Direct, full price.*
4. **Missions/clinics (Africa).** Zengamina ($3M church-raised) and Bondo precedents; a $3–8k unit is single-congregation fundable. Quiet, durable channel.

### Tier 1.5 — The regulatory unlock: US/Canada irrigation canals

**~150,000 miles of canals in 17 western US states + Alberta's districts; every check-drop is our site; FERC "qualifying conduit" ≤40 MW = no license, 45-day notice.** Precedents: Irrican (Alberta districts own 36 MW), Natel Monroe Drop, Emrgy ($18.4M raised — but targets 5–25 kW+, leaving sub-5 kW farm-ditch drops open). Farmer route stacks REAP (25–50%, currently frozen — monitor) + 30% ITC. *Payers: farmers (grant-assisted) and districts (fleet buyers). Caveat: canals run ~6 months (CF ~0.25–0.5) — price accordingly.*

## Structural findings that shape strategy

1. **Donor money is solar-gated.** Nearly every access program is written around solar+battery. Our wedge is arithmetic, not ideology: 24/7 hydro baseload cuts a hybrid mini-grid's battery bank (the #1 cost line) — quantify per-site and bid as *generation asset inside solar frameworks*, don't fight them.
2. **Anchor-first survives; community-only dies.** Ethiopia's cooperative plants: 3 of 5 dead. Zengamina (hospital), Mwenga (tea), MEGA (tea town): alive. Every deployment should have an anchor customer whose opex pays the freight.
3. **Two price architectures, one machine.** Western-margin unit ($3–8k installed) for NA/lodges/estates; localized-BOM program unit (<$1,500) for Asia/program channels later. Don't blend them.
4. **The regulatory gift:** man-made conduits (irrigation canals, hatchery raceways) bypass the worst of hydro permitting in the US entirely. The product's natural habitat is also its легal fast lane.
5. **Grid encroachment is the long-term tax** on household markets everywhere (killed Vietnam, stranding Nepal MHPs) — commercial anchors and diesel-cost arbitrage age better than household access plays.

## Recommended sequencing (both routes, as directed)

- **Residential route:** NA off-grid direct via existing hydro dealers; own the vacated Home Power content niche; Oregon/Montana/BC first (permitting ease).
- **Commercial route:** (1) NA hatcheries + irrigation-canal farms (conduit exemption; REAP when open); (2) Canadian lodges + Wah-ila-toos/REACHE pipeline started now for 2027–28 awards; (3) Kenya tea (KTDA engineering contact) as first export anchor; (4) file into Nepal IWM category and Philippines MSP frameworks as the program plays.
