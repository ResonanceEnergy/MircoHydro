**Date:** 2026-07-19 · **Trigger:** founder directive after the implosion/vortex evidence file surfaced GWVPP as working Schauberger-descended engineering. · **Sources:** 2023 systematic review (36 installed systems), vendor pages (Turbulent, Zotlöterer, Aquazoom, Kourispower), field studies. ## What they are, measured

Concrete basin (conical beats cylindrical, but 67% build cylindrical for cost) + inlet channel + free-surface vortex + slow rotor. **Installed base ~36 systems worldwide, mostly Austria/Europe, average under 5 kW, largest single basin ~20 kW.** Field efficiencies **29–85%, two-thirds below 60%**; lab optima 70–75% rarely survive to field. Head band **0.6–3.2 m — squarely overlapping ours.** Vendor floors: Turbulent 15 kW min / **1,500 L/s minimum flow**; Aquazoom ~1,000 L/s; Zotlöterer reference 900 L/s. ROI claims 2–8 yr; Zotlöterer 2005 build ≈ €7,200/kW before the press-release "$1/W" lore.

## Strategic rulings this audit supports

1. **Our core niche is physically uncontested.** No vortex basin can make useful power from 15–180 L/s — the flow floor is physics (P = ρgHQ leaves nothing at our flows and their efficiencies), not engineering effort. Nobody in the GWVPP industry can follow us down to ram-scale water. The sub-100 L/s low-head site class — the "pico counts as zero" gap we chose — stays ours.
2. **Do NOT pitch head-to-head on high-flow canals (>300 L/s at 1–3 m).** That is GWVPP home turf with a decade of receipts; Turbulent's Idaho canal project shows they're entering North America. Our canal/energy-waste play (THE_WINDOW) must be re-scoped to **low-flow laterals, spillway seeps, and structure-prohibited reaches** — or partnership, not competition, on the big drops.
3. **Cold climate is our sharpest edge against them and it's structural**, not incremental: an open vortex basin in a Ninth-of-March Alberta freeze-up versus our buried/insulated closed loop. Every northern-market document should carry this contrast (with their unproven-ice-claim status cited, not asserted).
4. **The fish-friendly battleground is an evidence race we can win.** Their flagship environmental claim rests on vendor assertion plus contested lab work; our stewardship spec commits to *measured* discharge deltas. First mover with instrumented water-quality data owns the regulator conversation.
5. **Machine E (vortex) — parked, honestly.** The physics works and is proven at 5–20 kW where ≥1 m³/s exists, but the concrete basin contradicts our appliance thesis (REQ-P1 zero concrete), and that market has incumbents. If we ever chase high-flow sites, licensing/partnering beats building. Recorded as an option, not a roadmap item.
6. **The Schauberger connection cuts our way.** GWVPP proves vortex hydro is bankable engineering at 40–73% measured — which retroactively legitimizes the vortex/tangential elements *we already ratified* (D-8 egg, tangential entry) while confirming none of it requires new physics. Useful narrative for grant reviewers who will Google "vortex."

**Net: the audit strengthens the founding position.** The one proven Schauberger-descended power industry lives exactly one flow decade above us, needs the concrete we banned, freezes where we winterize, and cannot deliver water. Their existence validates low-head vortex engineering to funders; their physics floor guarantees they never meet us at our sites.
