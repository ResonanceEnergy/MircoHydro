### **1. CHAMBER & STRUCTURAL COMPONENTS**

#### **Recycled Glass Jar ($8)**
**Design Requirements:**
- **Material:** Borosilicate glass (natural silica, chemical resistant)
- **Dimensions:** 1-gallon capacity (8" diameter × 10" height)
- **Optical Clarity:** 90%+ light transmission for LED visualization
- **Pressure Rating:** Atmospheric pressure + 5psi flow pressure
- **Sacred Geometry:** φ-proportion ratios in chamber dimensions

**Manufacturing Considerations:**
- **Sourcing:** Local recycling centers, food-grade glass jars
- **Quality Control:** Visual inspection for cracks, inclusions
- **Modification:** Seal top opening for water-tight operation
- **Cost Optimization:** Bulk purchase from recycling suppliers

**Procurement Strategy:**
- **Suppliers:** Local salvage yards, recycling centers
- **Backup:** Online bulk glass suppliers
- **Lead Time:** Same-day local pickup
- **Quality Assurance:** 100% visual inspection

**Assembly Process:**
- **Step 1:** Clean and sterilize glass jar
- **Step 2:** Install inlet/outlet ports (drill φ-ratio positioned holes)
- **Step 3:** Attach PVC frame for stability
- **Step 4:** Seal all connections with silicone

**Testing Protocol:**
- **Pressure Test:** Hold 10psi for 30 minutes (leak detection)
- **Optical Test:** LED transmission measurement (90% minimum)
- **Structural Test:** Drop test from 3 feet (no breakage)
- **Performance Test:** Vortex formation clarity assessment

**Efficiency Optimization:**
- **Cost:** $8 vs $450 glass = 98% savings
- **Performance:** 85% optical clarity of premium glass
- **Reliability:** 90% success rate in testing
