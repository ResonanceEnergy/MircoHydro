## 🔄 MATERIAL PHILOSOPHY & NATURAL CONSIDERATIONS

### **Acrylic vs Borosilicate Glass**
| Aspect | Acrylic (Budget) | Borosilicate Glass (Ideal) |
|--------|------------------|----------------------------|
| **Natural Origin** | Synthetic polymer (PMMA) | Silica-based (like quartz) |
| **Optical Clarity** | 92% light transmission | 95%+ light transmission |
| **Chemical Resistance** | Good for water | Excellent for water |
| **Energetic Properties** | Neutral synthetic | Natural mineral resonance |
| **Cost** | $45 (8" cube) | $450 (12" cube) |
| **Machinability** | Easy to cut/drill | Requires specialized equipment |

### **Natural Material Alternatives**
1. **Quartz Crystal:** Natural silica, expensive ($200+)
2. **Borosilicate Glass:** Most "natural" option, higher cost
3. **Polycarbonate:** More natural than acrylic, moderate cost ($80)
4. **Local Glass:** Montevideo artisan glass, potentially natural

### **Biomimetic Alignment**
- **Schauberger Principle:** "Work with nature's materials"
- **Recommendation:** Start with acrylic for proof-of-concept, upgrade to glass for production
- **Validation:** Core biomimetic principles can be proven with either material
